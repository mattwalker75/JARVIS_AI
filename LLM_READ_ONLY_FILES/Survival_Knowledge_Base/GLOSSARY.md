# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms used across the guides; each notes where to read more. Where the same word means two different things in two different guides (e.g., "kindling" a fire vs. a doe rabbit "kindling"), both meanings are listed separately.

---

## A

**AC (Alternating Current)** — the type of electricity that comes out of wall outlets and generators; used by most household appliances. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Access and functional needs (AFN)** — the standard emergency-management term (FEMA/CERT) for anyone who may need extra help before, during, or after a disaster: people with disabilities, older adults, children, people with limited English, and people without transportation or support. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Acute stress reaction** — the normal, temporary fight-flight-freeze response to danger (racing heart, tunnel vision, shaking); not a disorder, and it usually passes within minutes to hours once safety is restored. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Adjuster (claims adjuster)** — the insurance company's representative (or an independently hired one) who inspects damage and estimates or determines what the insurer will pay. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md), [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**AED (Automated External Defibrillator)** — a public-access device that reads heart rhythm and can shock a stopped or chaotic heart back into a normal rhythm; it talks you through every step, so use one immediately if available. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**AFCI (Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter)** — a breaker that detects the electrical "arcing" signature of a damaged wire or cord and trips before it can start a fire; often combined with GFCI protection in newer panels. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**After-action review (AAR)** — a structured look back at what happened, what worked, what failed, and what to change next time, borrowed from military and emergency-management practice. *See:* [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Amateur ("Ham") radio** — a licensed radio service (requires passing a written exam) that offers many bands, high power, repeater networks, and long-distance capability; the backbone of volunteer emergency communications in most U.S. disasters. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Amatoxins** — the toxin family found in death cap and destroying angel mushrooms; they attack the liver and kidneys and cause symptoms only after a long, dangerous delay. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Amend / amendment** — material worked into soil to improve it, such as compost, sand, manure, or peat/coir. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Amp-hour (Ah)** — how battery capacity is usually labeled (especially car and deep-cycle batteries); multiply Ah by voltage to get watt-hours (e.g., 100 Ah × 12V = 1,200 Wh). *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Analgesic** — a pain-relieving medicine. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Anaphylaxis** — a sudden, severe, whole-body allergic reaction that can close the airway and drop blood pressure; it can kill within minutes and is treated with epinephrine. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Anneal** — to soften hardened steel by heating it and letting it cool very slowly (e.g., buried in ash), so it can be filed, drilled, or forged without cracking. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Antipyretic** — a fever-reducing medicine (e.g., acetaminophen, ibuprofen). *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Antivenom** — the real medical treatment for a venomous bite: a lab-made antibody product given in a hospital. Nothing done in the field cures a venomous bite; first aid only buys time to reach antivenom. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**APGAR-style check** — a quick look at a newborn's breathing, color, and activity in the first minutes of life. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Apparent temperature** — the technical name for what the Heat Index and Wind Chill actually calculate: not the thermometer reading, but what a human body experiences given humidity or wind. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**AQI (Air Quality Index)** — a 0–500 EPA scale for outdoor air pollution, most relevant here for wildfire smoke (fine particles, "PM2.5"); above roughly 150 is unhealthy for everyone. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Attraction vs. location signals** — attraction signals (fire, whistle, mirror flashes) get a searcher's attention; location signals (ground symbols, bright clothing laid out) confirm exactly where you are once attention is on your area. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Authorized pickup list** — the list on file with a school or daycare of exactly who may take a child, requiring photo ID to release the child to anyone else. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

## B

**Backdraft / flashover (house fire)** — sudden, extremely dangerous fire behavior when a starved, smoldering fire gets a rush of new oxygen (e.g., opening a hot door) and instantly ignites explosively; this is why you check doors before opening them. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Backfeed / backfeeding** — electricity flowing backward from a generator through house wiring, out through the meter, and onto the utility's lines — the opposite of normal flow, and a leading cause of utility-worker deaths. Never plug a generator into a wall outlet without a transfer switch or interlock. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md), [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Backflow / sewer backup** — sewage pushed backward up through drains or toilets, usually because the main sewer line is blocked or overwhelmed (common in floods). *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Backing wind** — wind shifting counter-clockwise (e.g., S → SE → E), usually signaling an approaching low-pressure system and worsening weather. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Bag of waters / amniotic sac** — the fluid-filled membrane around a baby in the womb; "water breaking" is this sac rupturing. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Bank line** — thin, strong cord (like tarred nylon mason's line) used for trotlines, gorge-hook rigs, and snares; a small coil is extremely valuable survival gear. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Banking a fire** — covering hot coals with ash to slow their burn so they survive for hours (often overnight) and can be revived later by uncovering and adding air and tinder. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Barometric pressure** — the weight of the air above you; falling pressure means air is rising (clouds, storms forming), rising pressure means air is sinking (clearing skies). *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Barter** — trading a good or service directly for another good or service without cash changing hands (though a trade can be part-cash, part-goods). *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Base layer** — the clothing layer against your skin, whose job is to move ("wick") sweat away from your body rather than to keep you warm. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Batoning** — striking the spine of a knife with a stick or club to split wood, instead of swinging the blade. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Bearing (azimuth)** — a direction expressed in degrees, 0–360°, measured clockwise from north (000° = north, 090° = east, 180° = south, 270° = west). *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Benchmark good** — a common, stable, easy-to-compare item (a AA battery, a bottle of water, a roll of toilet paper) people informally use as a mental "unit" to compare very different items during a trade. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**"Best by" / "use by" date** — the manufacturer's estimate of peak quality, not always a hard safety cliff for shelf-stable dry goods. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Bevel / edge angle** — the angled surface ground onto a cutting edge; a smaller angle is sharper but more fragile, a larger one is tougher but cuts worse. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md), [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Bight** — a U-shaped bend in a rope where the two sides don't cross. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Biosecurity** — practices that keep disease out of your animals: quarantine, clean boots and tools, and controlling contact with wild birds, rodents, and other people's animals. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Bitter end** — the very tip of a rope's working end. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Blackland Prairie** — the dark, clay-rich soil ecoregion covering Collin County; fertile, but it turns to deep, sticky mud when wet and cracks hard when dry. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Blackwater / black water** — water contaminated with human feces, sewage, or floodwater mixed with sewer overflow; always treat it as infectious, never as "just dirty water." *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md), [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Block captain / point person** — a volunteer (not a boss) who keeps the neighborhood roster, runs meetings, and relays messages; a coordination role, not an authority, and one that should rotate. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Blue-green algae bloom** — a toxic bacterial bloom common in warm, nutrient-rich (fertilizer/manure runoff) still water in summer; can poison people and pets even after boiling, for some toxin types. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Boil-water notice / advisory** — an official order from a water utility that tap water may be unsafe and must be boiled or otherwise disinfected before drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, or making ice. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Bolting** — a plant switching to flowering/seed production early (usually from heat or long days), which makes leaves bitter and tough. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Botulism** — a rare but often fatal paralytic illness from a toxin made by *Clostridium botulinum* growing in sealed, low-oxygen, low-acid conditions (improperly canned food, home-fermented oils); spores survive boiling and only pressure-canning temperatures (240–250°F) reliably kill them. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Bottleneck** — a single choke point (one bridge, one two-lane road) that an entire evacuating population must funnel through, a common cause of gridlock. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Bow drill / hand drill** — primitive friction-fire methods that create an ember by spinning a wooden spindle against a wooden fireboard until friction-generated dust ignites. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Brain-tanned leather** — hide that has been chemically softened (traditionally with a brain-and-water emulsion), worked to stay soft after drying, and usually smoked for water resistance. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Branch circuit / breaker** — one of the smaller switches in a breaker panel, feeding a specific area or set of outlets/lights (e.g., "Kitchen," "Bedrooms"). *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**Breaker panel / service panel / load center ("the box")** — the metal box where power enters the house and is split into individual circuits, usually in a garage, basement, utility closet, or exterior wall. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Breech** — a baby positioned to come out feet-, knees-, or buttocks-first instead of head-first; needs professional help if at all possible. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Broiler** — a chicken raised specifically for meat, grown fast and butchered young (8–12 weeks). *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Brood / brood box / super** — brood is developing bees (egg → larva → pupa) sealed under wax capping; the brood box is the lower hive box where the queen lays and the colony lives, versus the honey super added above it purely to store surplus honey for harvest. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Broody** — a hen that wants to sit on eggs to hatch them; she stops laying and sits on the nest. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Buddy system** — pairing a younger or less capable person with an older one so no one is ever unaccounted for without someone noticing quickly. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

**Buffer / safety margin** — the number of days of a resource you want to always keep in reserve, below which you take action rather than wait and see. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Bug-out bag (BOB) / go-bag** — a bag packed in advance with everything needed to survive 72 hours or more away from home, grabbed on the way out the door. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md), [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md), [Disaster-Specific Guides](topics/disaster-specific/disaster-specific.md).

**Burn rate** — how much fuel (propane, gasoline, kerosene, wood) a stove, heater, or generator uses per hour of operation. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Burr (wire edge)** — a thin curl of metal pushed to the back of an edge during sharpening; feeling for it tells you a bevel is fully ground. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md), [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Butterfat / cream** — the fat portion of milk, lighter than the rest, which rises to the top if milk sits undisturbed; churned cream becomes butter. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Buttermilk** — the liquid left over after churning cream into butter (traditional buttermilk), distinct from the thicker, tangier cultured buttermilk sold in stores, though both can be used as a starter culture. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

## C

**CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)** — a rating of how much filtered air a portable air cleaner puts out per minute, used to size a purifier to a room. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Calorie (kcal)** — the unit of food energy printed on nutrition labels as "Calories"; adults typically need roughly 1,600–3,000 kcal/day depending on age, size, sex, and activity level. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Caloric density** — how many calories a food provides per unit of weight or volume; matters for how much space and weight a food stockpile takes. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Capillary action** — the way a liquid climbs up a narrow fiber or gap against gravity — how a candle wick "pulls" fuel to the flame without being pumped. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Capped / ripe honey** — honey the bees have dried to about 17–18% moisture and sealed under white wax capping; the only honey safe to harvest and store long-term, since uncapped honey is too wet and will ferment. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Carbon monoxide (CO)** — a colorless, odorless, tasteless gas from incomplete combustion (generators, gas appliances, engines, charcoal, wood stoves) that replaces oxygen in your blood; called "the silent killer" because it gives no warning. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md), [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md), [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Carbon / tool steel** — steel with enough carbon (roughly 0.5%+) to respond to hardening and tempering; found in old files, many leaf springs, and older saw blades. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Catching feature (backstop)** — a large, hard-to-miss feature (a highway, a river, a lakeshore) beyond your target that tells you "if you hit this, you've gone too far — turn back." *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Cat-hole** — a small, individual hole dug for a single use of feces, then covered immediately; the basic wilderness/short-term sanitation method. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**Category 1/2/3 water** — a water-damage classification: Category 1 is clean (a broken supply line), Category 2 ("grey water") has some contamination, Category 3 ("black water") is grossly contaminated (sewage, floodwater, storm surge) and must be treated as hazardous; most disaster flooding is Category 2 or 3. *See:* [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**CB (Citizens Band) radio** — a license-free, 40-channel radio service around 27 MHz, popular with truckers and off-roaders, with a few miles of range depending on terrain and antenna. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Cellulitis** — a spreading bacterial skin infection: redness, warmth, and swelling spreading outward. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**CERT (Community Emergency Response Team)** — FEMA's standardized model and training course for how ordinary residents organize to help their own neighborhood in the hours before professional responders arrive. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Chain of custody / provenance** — a simple, honest record of who found a deceased person, when, where, what was done, and by whom — what lets authorities and family trust what happened later. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Char / char cloth** — char is blackened, partially burned material that holds a glowing ember without flaming; char cloth is cotton fabric slow-cooked with no oxygen until black but still woven, which catches the faintest spark and is one of the best tinder materials there is. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Charge controller** — a device between a solar panel and a battery that prevents overcharging and damage; required for any solar setup bigger than a small trickle charger. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Chilblains** — painful, itchy, red/purple skin damage from repeated cold-but-not-freezing exposure, especially with dampness. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Chimney (lamp)** — the glass tube around an oil lamp's flame that improves airflow, making the flame brighter and steadier and protecting it from wind or drafts. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Circuit** — one branch of house wiring serving a specific set of outlets, lights, or appliances, protected by its own breaker (or fuse in older homes). *See:* [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Circumpolar / pole star** — a star that appears to stay in roughly the same spot while other stars wheel around it, because it sits near the sky's apparent axis of rotation; in the Northern Hemisphere, that's Polaris. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Clabber** — milk that has naturally soured and thickened from its own bacteria (no added culture), common with fresh raw milk left at warm room temperature. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Clean break** — the test for whether rennet-set curd is ready to cut: a clean finger or knife slides in and splits the curd along a sharp line instead of dragging through soft custard-like curd. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Cold front** — cold, dense air pushing under warm air and forcing it up quickly, producing sharp temperature drops, gusty wind shifts, and often a line of thunderstorms. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Cold process (CP) / hot process (HP) soap** — cold process mixes fat and lye solution at moderate temperature and lets the reaction finish slowly in the mold over weeks; hot process cooks the batter after trace so saponification finishes in hours instead of weeks. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Cold smoking vs. hot smoking** — cold smoking (below about 90°F) flavors and helps preserve food but does not cook it, so the food must be fully cooked or cured separately; hot smoking (225–275°F) both cooks and flavors. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Colony** — the whole bee family living in one hive: one queen, thousands of female workers, and (seasonally) male drones. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Comb** — the six-sided wax cells bees build inside the hive to store honey and pollen and raise young. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Comfort item** — a small, familiar object (stuffed animal, blanket scrap, favorite toy) that helps regulate a child's stress, carried in the go-bag specifically for that purpose. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

**Compassion fatigue** — the exhaustion helpers feel from repeatedly supporting others in distress; a real risk for anyone playing a caretaker role for days or weeks. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Complete protein** — a protein source containing all nine essential amino acids in adequate amounts (meat, fish, eggs, dairy, soy); most single plant foods are incomplete alone but become complete combined with a complementary plant food. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**Compost** — decomposed organic matter (food scraps, leaves, manure) turned into rich, dark soil food. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Conduction** — heat loss or gain through direct contact, such as lying on cold ground or a hot floor radiating heat upward. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Confirming / pronouncing death** — the formal medical act of determining that death has occurred, normally done by a paramedic, physician, medical examiner, or coroner — not a bystander; in many rural Texas counties a Justice of the Peace performs this role. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Consensus / rough consensus** — a decision most people actively agree with and no one strongly blocks, reached by discussion rather than a vote or an order. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Consumption rate** — how much of something you use per unit of time, usually per day (e.g., gallons of water per day for a household). *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Contaminant** — anything harmful in water: biological (germs, killed by boiling/disinfection), chemical (pesticides, fuel, heavy metals — not removed by boiling or bleach), or physical (mud, silt, debris — removed by settling and filtering). *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md).

**Contamination** — hazardous material physically present on a person, object, food, or water; a physical problem you deal with by removing, diluting, or blocking it, not by waiting it out. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Continuity of care** — keeping a chronic condition (diabetes, asthma, heart disease, dementia) stable when the normal medical routine is disrupted. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md), [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Contraction** — a tightening/cramping of the uterus during labor, timed from the start of one to the start of the next. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Contraflow** — emergency traffic management (usually for hurricanes) where all lanes, including normally inbound ones, are turned to flow outbound, away from danger. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Contraindication** — a reason not to use a drug or remedy: an allergy, another condition, a drug interaction, or an age restriction. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Contrast** — how much a signal stands out from its background (color, brightness, shape); the core principle behind almost every rescue-signaling method. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Convection** — heat carried away by moving air (wind chill) or water; a wet body loses heat fast in moving air or water. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Cord prolapse** — the umbilical cord slipping out ahead of the baby during birth; a true emergency because it cuts off the baby's oxygen. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Cordage** — the general term for rope, string, or twine, especially when hand-made from natural fiber. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Core temperature** — the temperature deep inside your body (as opposed to your skin); this is what actually determines medical danger, in both heat and cold. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md), [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Corrosive** — a chemical that chemically burns or destroys tissue on contact, such as strong acids or strong bases (drain cleaner, bleach concentrate). *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Corsi-Rosenthal box (CR box)** — a DIY air cleaner made from a box fan and 4–5 furnace filters taped into a cube; a low-cost, near-HEPA-equivalent cleaner for a room. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**"Cotton kills"** — a survival saying (backed by NIOSH/CDC cold-stress guidance) that cotton absorbs and holds many times its weight in water, dries slowly, and loses almost all insulating value when wet, while wool, silk, and most synthetics keep most of their warmth even wet. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Cover material** — dry material (sawdust, dry leaves, ash, dirt, kitty litter, lime) added after each latrine use to reduce smell, flies, and visible waste. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**CPR (Cardiopulmonary resuscitation)** — chest compressions (and optionally rescue breaths) that manually pump blood when the heart has stopped, keeping the brain alive until the heart can be restarted. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Cross Timbers** — the belt of oak/juniper woodland just west of the blackland prairie; Collin County sits mostly in blackland prairie with some Cross Timbers influence to the west. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Crowning** — the moment a baby's head is visible and stays visible at the vaginal opening between contractions — birth is imminent, minutes away. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**CTCSS / "PL tone"** — a quiet sub-audible tone added to a radio transmission so a repeater (or another radio) only opens up for signals carrying the right tone; it is not privacy or encryption, since anyone without tone squelch can still hear you. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Culling** — removing (by selling, rehoming, or butchering) an animal that is unproductive, sick, or aggressive, to protect the health and resources of the rest. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Culture / starter** — a small amount of a live-bacteria product (yogurt, buttermilk, a powdered starter, kefir grains) added to milk to grow a known, food-safe bacteria population that gives a dairy product its flavor and texture. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Curb stop** — the water shutoff valve located outside near the property line, usually needing a special key/tool, that belongs to the utility and serves as a backup to your interior shutoff. *See:* [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md), [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Curds and whey** — when milk is clotted by acid, rennet, or both, it separates into curds (the solid protein/fat clot that becomes cheese) and whey (the leftover thin, yellow-green liquid). *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Curing (meat/fish)** — preserving meat or fish with salt (and often nitrite/nitrate) to lower water activity and inhibit bacteria. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Curing (soap)** — the weeks-long resting period after unmolding a cold-process soap bar, during which it loses water, hardens, and the last of the lye finishes reacting, making the bar milder and longer-lasting. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

## D

**Danger zone** — the 40°F–140°F temperature range where bacteria multiply fastest in food; food should spend as little time in this range as possible. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Darning** — rebuilding a hole in fabric (especially knits) by weaving new thread across the gap, rather than covering it with a patch. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Days remaining** — how many more days a resource will last at its current rate of use: days remaining = amount left ÷ daily use rate. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Days to maturity (DTM)** — how many days from planting (or transplanting) until you can harvest a crop, printed on seed packets. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Dead reckoning** — figuring your position by tracking the direction traveled and distance covered from a known starting point, without landmarks. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Deadfall trap** — a trap that uses a weight (a log or rock) to crush or pin an animal, triggered by a disturbed support stick. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Decedent** — the formal/legal term for a person who has died, seen on paperwork and used by officials. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Decision trigger** — a pre-agreed, specific condition that automatically means a specific action (e.g., "if water reaches the third porch step, we leave"), decided calmly in advance so you don't debate it in the moment. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Decomposition** — the natural breakdown of a body after death, which speeds up sharply in heat and humidity and slows sharply in cold. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Decontamination ("decon")** — the process of removing contamination from a person, object, or area, most often by removing clothing and rinsing with water; removing outer clothing alone removes up to about 90% of contamination. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Deductible** — the amount you pay out of pocket before insurance starts paying on a claim. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Deed** — the legal document proving who owns a piece of real property (land or house). *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Deep-cycle battery** — a battery designed to be drained and recharged repeatedly, unlike a car starter battery designed for a brief high-current burst. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**De-energize / energize** — turn power off / turn power on; professionals say "de-energize" because "off" can be ambiguous about which switch is meant. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**De-escalation** — verbal and behavioral techniques (calm voice, open hands, distance, giving an "out") used to lower the tension of a confrontation so it doesn't turn violent. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Defensible space** — a cleared, maintained buffer zone around a structure (typically 30–100+ feet) with reduced vegetation, used to slow or stop an advancing wildfire or grassfire. *See:* [Disaster-Specific Guides](topics/disaster-specific/disaster-specific.md).

**Deficiency disease** — an illness caused specifically by lack of one nutrient over time, not by lack of calories overall. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**DEET** — an insect repellent applied to exposed skin (and some clothing) that repels, but doesn't kill, mosquitoes and ticks. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Dew point** — the temperature at which air becomes saturated and moisture condenses; a close dew point/temperature spread (humid air) fuels stronger thunderstorms. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Diaphoresis** — heavy, often cold and clammy sweating; a symptom seen in heat exhaustion and some heart emergencies. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Direct sow** — planting a seed straight into its final growing spot, instead of starting it elsewhere and transplanting it. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Disaster Recovery Center (DRC)** — a temporary, in-person FEMA/state site set up after a major disaster where you can apply for assistance, ask questions, and get help uploading documents. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Disinfect** — to kill germs in water using heat or chemicals. *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md).

**Dispatch** — the humane killing of an animal for meat. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Doe / buck / wether** — female / male terms for rabbits and goats; a wether is a castrated male goat. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Document kit / go-kit** — a portable folder or bag holding copies of your most important papers, ready to grab during an evacuation. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Donut / space-saver spare** — a small, skinny temporary spare tire rated for short distances at reduced speed only (usually 50 mph / up to about 70 miles — check the sidewall). *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**DRABC / ABC** — a memorized order for checking an injured person: Danger (to you), Response (are they conscious), Airway (open?), Breathing (normal?), Circulation (pulse, severe bleeding). *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Dressing a knot** — snugging all wraps of a knot so they lie neat and flat before loading it; a messy knot is weaker and can slip. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

## E

**EAS (Emergency Alert System)** — the system that lets national, state, and local officials interrupt TV and radio broadcasts with emergency messages. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**East Fork Trinity River** — the river that drains most of Collin County, flowing south/southeast into Lavon Lake and eventually Lake Ray Hubbard downstream. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Egress** — a way out of a structure; always keep one clear and known before going further into a damaged building. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Elapid** — the snake family that includes coral snakes: fixed, shorter fangs and a neurotoxic (nerve-affecting) venom, unlike a pit viper's tissue-damaging venom. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Electrolytes** — salts (sodium, potassium, chloride) the body needs in the right balance to function, lost heavily during vomiting, diarrhea, and heavy sweating. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Ember** — a glowing (but not flaming) coal of very hot material that must be nurtured into an open flame by blowing air into a tinder bundle. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Encryption** — scrambling digital data so it can't be read without a password or key; often already built into modern phones and computers. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**ERCOT** — the Texas electric grid operator; because Texas's grid is largely isolated from other states, widespread outages (like February 2021) can last longer with less outside help. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**ERP (effective radiated power)** — the FCC's measure of a radio transmitter's legal power limit, accounting for antenna gain rather than just the radio's wattage. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Evacuate / evacuation** — leaving your current location, before or during a disaster, because staying is more dangerous than moving; evacuating early is always safer than evacuating late. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md), [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md), [Disaster-Specific Guides](topics/disaster-specific/disaster-specific.md).

**Evaporation / evaporative cooling** — the cooling effect when a liquid (sweat or wet cloth) turns to vapor, pulling heat from the skin; the body's main cooling system, and dangerous in cold since wet fabric chills you fast. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md), [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Excess Flow Valve (EFV) / seismic shutoff valve** — an automatic valve on some gas meters that shuts gas off by itself if flow suddenly surges (a broken line) or the ground shakes hard; if you find gas already off and didn't shut it yourself, this may be why. *See:* [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Exclusion** — sealing up a structure so pests (rodents, insects) physically cannot get in, as opposed to poisoning or trapping them after they're already inside. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Exposure (hazmat)** — contact with a hazard through a route of entry: skin, lungs, mouth, or a wound. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

## F

**Fallout** — radioactive dust and debris that settles out of the air after a nuclear detonation, dangerous mainly through external exposure and if inhaled, ingested, or left on skin. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Family Communication Plan** — the written document listing contacts, rally points, roles, and key account/medical information for a household during an emergency. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Fatwood** ("fat lighter" / "lighter'd") — resin-soaked wood from pine stumps and knots, full of flammable sap; it lights even when damp, almost like a candle. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Fecal-oral route** — the way disease spreads: germs from feces get onto hands, food, water, or objects, then into another person's mouth. Breaking this chain is the whole point of sanitation and handwashing. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**FEMA Individual Assistance** — federal disaster aid for individuals and households, available only after a federal disaster declaration for your county, generally only for needs insurance doesn't cover. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md), [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Ferrocerium rod ("ferro rod" / "fire steel")** — a manufactured metal alloy rod that, scraped hard with a sharp edge, throws sparks around 3,000°C — hot enough to ignite proper tinder even when wet. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**FIFO (First In, First Out)** — a rotation rule where the oldest stock is placed to be used first and new stock goes behind or under it, so nothing quietly expires at the back of a shelf. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md), [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Field dressing** — removing an animal's internal organs promptly after a kill, to stop bacterial growth and cool the meat. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Figure-4 trigger** — a simple three-stick trigger mechanism, shaped like the numeral 4, that holds up a deadfall trap's weight until disturbed. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Filter** — to strain water through material to remove particles, and, with carbon, some chemicals or taste. *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md).

**Fire lay** — the physical shape you build with tinder, kindling, and fuel before lighting a fire (teepee, log cabin, lean-to, etc.). *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Fire triangle** — the three things every fire needs at once: heat (an ignition source), fuel (something that burns), and oxygen (air); firefighters "break the triangle" to put fires out. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Fish weir** — a funnel-shaped fence or wall built across a stream that channels fish into a narrowing trap or holding pen. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Fixture shutoff / stop valve** — a small valve under a sink, behind a toilet, or at a washing machine that shuts off water to just that one fixture without affecting the rest of the house. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Flash flood** — a rapid rise of normally small or dry creeks after heavy rain, extremely dangerous on flat, hard-clay prairie ground because water spreads fast and low-water crossings flood before drivers realize it. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md), [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Flash point** — the lowest temperature at which a liquid's vapors can ignite; low flash point fuels (gasoline, alcohol) flare up easily, high flash point fuels (kerosene, tallow) are safer for a slow open-wick lamp. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Flashover / spot fire (wildfire)** — when wildfire conditions cause fire to jump ahead of itself via flying embers, common in dry grass and wind. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Flow / nectar flow** — a period when flowering plants produce enough nectar for bees to gather surplus beyond their own needs; the only time honey supers should go on a hive. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Flux** — a substance that keeps oxygen off hot metal so a joint can bond: borax for forge-welding, paste or liquid flux for solder and braze. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Forage / foraging (wild food)** — gathering wild, uncultivated plants, fungi, or insects for food. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Forage / browse (livestock feed)** — plants animals eat directly from the land; grass is forage, while brush, leaves, and weeds are browse, which goats prefer. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Force continuum / proportionality** — the legal and ethical principle that any response to a threat should be the minimum necessary to stay safe; "standing your ground" over property is treated very differently by law than protecting your life. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Forging moves** — the basic hammer techniques at the forge: drawing out (thinning/lengthening), upsetting (thickening/shortening), bending, and punching a hole. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Fortification** — nutrients added to a food during processing (iodized salt, fortified flour, vitamin D milk); checking labels for this quietly prevents several deficiency diseases. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**Foundation** — the base a house sits on: a concrete slab, a crawlspace with a perimeter footing, or a full basement. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Frame** — a removable wooden or plastic rectangle that hangs inside a beehive box, on which bees build comb; frames let you inspect, move, and harvest without destroying the nest. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Front** — the leading edge of a large air mass, a boundary where warm and cold air meet; crossing fronts causes most weather changes. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Frost date** — the average date of the last spring frost and first fall frost for your area; the safe planting window for tender crops sits between them. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Frostbite** — actual freezing of skin and possibly deeper tissue, which can cause permanent damage or loss of the affected part. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Frostnip** — mild, reversible skin cooling or freezing with no permanent damage. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**FRS (Family Radio Service)** — short-range, license-free two-way "walkie talkie" radios, typically good for real-world ranges of a few hundred feet to about a mile. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Fuel wood** — wrist-thick branches and larger logs that sustain a fire and produce lasting heat, though they won't light directly from a match or spark. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Fundus** — the top of the uterus, felt as a firm mass in the lower abdomen after birth; massaging it helps it contract and control bleeding. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

## G

**Game trail** — a worn path through vegetation used repeatedly by animals moving between bedding, water, and feeding areas, identifiable by tracks, droppings, and matted grass. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Get-home bag / stay bag** — a smaller bag kept at work, school, or in a vehicle, built to get you back home (or to your family) if your normal transportation fails, possibly on foot. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**GFCI (Ground-Fault Circuit Interrupter)** — a special outlet or breaker that detects current leaking to ground (such as through water or a person) and shuts off in a fraction of a second; required near water in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, and outdoors. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Ghee** — butter simmered until all the water evaporates and the milk solids are removed, leaving pure, shelf-stable butterfat. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**GMRS (General Mobile Radio Service)** — a radio band similar to FRS but with higher power, larger antennas, and repeater use allowed; requires a paid FCC license (no test) that covers your whole family. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Golden hour** — the informal idea that trauma outcomes are much better if a person reaches real surgical care within about an hour of a severe injury; a guideline, not a hard rule, but a reason speed matters. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Gorge hook** — a straight or slightly curved sliver of bone, wood, or metal, sharpened at both ends and tied in the middle to a fishing line, that lodges crosswise once swallowed. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Grab-and-go / bug-out kit** — a pre-packed, portable kit meant to be carried if you must leave immediately, drawn from a larger stationary stockpile. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Grain (fabric)** — the direction of the threads in fabric; cutting "on grain" keeps pieces stable, cutting "on the bias" (diagonally) gives stretch and drape. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Gray man / gray rock** — the practice of blending in and appearing unremarkable so you don't stand out as someone worth targeting, watching, or robbing. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Greywater / gray water** — used household water from washing dishes, laundry, or bathing that has not touched toilet waste; less dangerous than blackwater but still needs care. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md), [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Grid-down** — an extended loss of electrical power (hours to weeks) affecting heating/cooling, water pumping and treatment, refrigeration, communications, and fuel pumps. *See:* [Disaster-Specific Guides](topics/disaster-specific/disaster-specific.md).

**Grief** — the normal emotional response to loss (of a person, a home, a way of life), which can include sadness, anger, guilt, numbness, or relief, often in a shifting mix. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Grit** — abrasive coarseness in sharpening stones: low numbers (100–400) remove metal fast and fix damage, high numbers (1000–8000) polish. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Ground insulation / bedding** — a layer of dry material between your body and the ground or cold air that stops conductive heat loss; often the single most-overlooked survival item. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Grounding technique** — a simple sensory exercise (naming things you can see, hear, or touch) that pulls attention out of racing thoughts and back into the present moment. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Ground-to-air signal** — a large symbol built on open ground (branches, rocks, trampled snow, clothing) meant to be read from an aircraft. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

## H

**Half-value layer** — the thickness of a given material that cuts gamma radiation passing through it roughly in half; more thickness means more halvings and much less radiation getting through. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Handrail (navigation)** — a linear feature (road, ridge, fence line, power line, stream) you can follow or use to keep yourself oriented. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Harden (quench)** — heating carbon steel to its critical temperature, then cooling it fast in oil or water to make it hard and brittle. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Hardening (security)** — physically strengthening a structure's weak points (doors, windows, locks) so forcing entry takes longer, makes more noise, or becomes impractical. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Hardening off (plants)** — gradually exposing indoor-started seedlings to outdoor sun, wind, and temperature over 7–10 days before permanent transplant, so they don't die of shock. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Hazard flashers** — the four-way blinking lights on a vehicle, turned on with a button usually marked with a red triangle. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Hazmat (hazardous material)** — any substance that can harm health, safety, or property: industrial chemicals, fuels, pesticides, cleaning products, and radioactive material. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Heat exhaustion / heatstroke** — heat exhaustion is heavy sweating, weakness, and nausea while still alert; heatstroke is life-threatening, with sweating that may stop, hot/dry or clammy skin, and confusion or collapse, and needs emergency cooling and medical help immediately. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md), [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Heat index** — how hot it feels combining air temperature and humidity; humidity blocks sweat from evaporating, so it feels — and is — more dangerous than the thermometer alone suggests. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Heliograph / signal mirror** — a small reflective surface (mirror, glass, polished metal, phone screen) used to flash sunlight at a distant target such as an aircraft or ridge. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Hemotoxic venom** — venom that damages blood, blood vessels, and tissue near a bite (pit vipers), causing swelling, bruising, and local tissue damage. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**HEPA** — "High-Efficiency Particulate Air" filter, removing at least 99.97% of 0.3-micron particles; the gold standard for filtering smoke particles from air. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**High-acid food** — food with pH 4.6 or below (most fruit, pickles with enough vinegar, jams); safe for boiling-water-bath canning. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**High-centered** — when a vehicle's underside (not its tires) rests on the ground or an obstacle, lifting the drive wheels off the surface so they spin uselessly. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Hive** — the physical box or structure a bee colony lives in; the colony is the bees, the hive is the box. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Home inventory** — a room-by-room record (photos/video plus a list) of your belongings and their approximate value, used to support an insurance claim. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Homogenization** — a mechanical process that breaks milk fat into tiny, evenly distributed globules so cream doesn't rise; goat milk is naturally close to homogenized already. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Hot spot (blister)** — a spot of friction or rubbing on the foot that feels warm, tender, or reddened — the warning sign right before a blister forms. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Husbandry** — the day-to-day work of keeping animals healthy: feeding, watering, housing, breeding, and disease prevention. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Hyperthermia** — dangerously high core body temperature, the heat-illness family of which heat stroke is the most severe form. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Hypothermia** — dangerously low core body temperature from cold exposure; newborns, infants, and the elderly lose heat far faster than healthy adults. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md), [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md), [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

## I

**ICE contact** — an "In Case of Emergency" contact stored in a phone and written on paper, used by responders or strangers to reach your family. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Improvised alarm** — a simple, non-electronic early-warning device (cans on a string, a gravel path, a tripwire bell) that makes noise when someone approaches or enters, giving advance warning. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**In-kind trade** — the general term for trading "in goods" rather than in money. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Insulation** — trapped air is what actually keeps you warm; the material (down, wool, synthetic fill) just holds the air still. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Interlock device** — a simpler, cheaper mechanical part added to a breaker panel that physically prevents the main breaker and a generator breaker from both being switched on at once, preventing backfeed. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**Inverter** — a device that converts DC battery power into AC power so you can run normal household plugs off a battery or car. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Isolated / vulnerable neighbor** — someone at higher risk of being missed or unable to self-rescue: elderly people living alone, people with disabilities or mobility limits, people on home medical equipment, non-English speakers, single parents with no nearby family, and people without a vehicle. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

## J

**Jack / jack point / pinch weld** — the tool that lifts a car off the ground, and the reinforced spot under the car's rocker panel designed to hold it safely; using the wrong spot can dent the floor or let the car fall. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Jump pack / jump starter** — a self-contained battery pack that jump-starts a car without needing a second vehicle. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Jumper cables** — a pair of long cables with clamps used to transfer electrical current from a good battery to a dead one. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

## K

**Kidding** — a doe goat giving birth to "kids." *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Kindling (fire)** — small sticks, from pencil-thickness up to thumb-thickness, that catch from burning tinder and burn long and hot enough to light real fuel wood. *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Kindling (rabbit birth)** — a doe rabbit giving birth. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Knit fabric** — fabric made from one continuous thread looped through itself in rows (T-shirts, socks, sweaters); it stretches, and a cut or hole tends to "run" or ladder outward. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Kwashiorkor** — a protein-deficiency disease, distinct from the calorie-deficiency wasting of marasmus. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

## L

**Labor** — the process of contractions and cervix-opening that leads to birth, in three stages: dilation, delivery of the baby, and delivery of the placenta. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Lard** — rendered pig fat, softer than tallow but still good for making bar soap. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Lashing** — rope wrapped around two or more poles to bind them into a rigid structure, as opposed to a knot, which joins rope to itself or another rope. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Latrine** — a larger, dug pit designed for repeated or group use over days to weeks, with a cover and often a seat or slab. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**Layer (chicken)** — a chicken kept for egg production. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Layers of defense (defense in depth)** — the idea that no single barrier needs to stop everything; a series of barriers (perimeter, yard, doors, windows, interior room) each slow down or discourage a threat and buy time. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Ledger** — a written record of who traded what with whom and when. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**License by rule** — a radio service (FRS, MURS, CB) you're automatically authorized to use just by following the rules, with no application, fee, or test. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Load-bearing wall / element (structural)** — a part of a building that holds it up: a wall, beam, or post supporting part of the structure above; damage here is far more serious than to a non-structural wall. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md), [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Load-bearing vs. non-load-bearing (knots)** — whether a knot will hold body weight or heavy strain, requiring a strong, tested knot, versus light duty, where a quick knot is fine. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Lockdown plan** — a rehearsed, written plan for what every household member does the moment a security threat is identified: where to go, who calls whom, what to bring. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Look, Listen, Link** — the three-step Psychological First Aid model: assess safety and immediate needs (Look), be present without forcing disclosure (Listen), and connect the person to information, services, and social support (Link). *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Lookalike** — a different plant or mushroom that resembles an edible one closely enough to cause dangerous confusion. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Low-acid food** — food with pH above 4.6 (most vegetables, meats, poultry, seafood, dairy) that can support *Clostridium botulinum* growth in a sealed, oxygen-free jar and requires pressure canning. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Lug nuts** — the nuts holding a wheel onto a car, usually 4–6 per wheel. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Lye** — a strong alkali used to break fat down into soap: sodium hydroxide (store lye) makes hard bar soap, while potassium hydroxide (from leached wood ashes) makes soft/liquid soap. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

## M

**Macronutrients** — the nutrients that provide calories and energy: carbohydrates, fats, and protein. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**Magnetic declination** — the angle between true north and magnetic north at a given location; ignoring it can put you a mile or more off course over a long distance. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Magnetic north** — the direction a compass needle actually points, toward the Earth's shifting magnetic north pole. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Main breaker** — the single large switch (or ganged pair) at the top of a breaker panel that controls all power to the entire house at once. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**Main shutoff (valve/breaker)** — the single valve or switch that stops an entire utility (water, gas, or electricity) from entering the house, as opposed to a valve or breaker for just one fixture or room. *See:* [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md), [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Malnutrition** — health harm from too little food, or too little of specific nutrients, over time; a slower, sneakier danger than starvation, and one that can be caused by well-intentioned rationing that goes on too long or too deep. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md), [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Mammatus clouds** — pouch-like, hanging cloud formations usually seen on the underside of an anvil after a severe thunderstorm has already formed, marking a strong, mature storm. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Map Your Neighborhood (MYN)** — a structured program that walks a block of 15–20 households through an immediate disaster-response sequence, a shared skills/equipment inventory, and a map of utility shutoffs. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Mediation** — a neutral third person helping two disputing people talk it through and find a solution, without taking sides or issuing a ruling. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Medical examiner / coroner** — the official (often a physician, sometimes a Justice of the Peace in Texas) responsible for investigating a death, especially one that is sudden, violent, unexplained, or unattended by a doctor. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Mercaptan** — the "rotten egg" chemical added to naturally odorless natural gas and propane specifically so leaks can be smelled. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**MERV rating** — Minimum Efficiency Reporting Value (1–20), rating how well an HVAC/furnace filter traps particles; higher MERV means finer filtration, with MERV 13 the practical ceiling for most home systems. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Meter (electric/gas)** — the device, usually mounted outside, that measures how much electricity or gas a home uses; gas meters have a shutoff valve near them. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Micronutrients** — vitamins and minerals needed in small amounts, essential for the body to function; they provide no calories but prevent deficiency diseases. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**Microclimate** — the localized conditions around a specific chosen spot; a hollow can be 10–15°F colder at night than open ground nearby, and a shaded north-facing spot can be 20°F cooler than open sun. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Mild steel** — common low-carbon steel (most rebar, angle iron, hardware); easy to forge and won't crack, but cannot be hardened. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Mold remediation** — the process of safely removing mold growth and the conditions that caused it, as opposed to just painting over it. *See:* [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Molting** — the annual period, usually fall, when hens lose and regrow feathers and stop laying for 4–8 weeks. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Mordant** — a chemical, commonly alum, that helps natural dye bond permanently to fiber instead of washing out. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Morale** — a group's shared sense that effort is worthwhile and things can improve; it degrades under fatigue, unfairness, boredom, and uncertainty, but is rebuildable through routine, purpose, and small wins. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**MURS (Multi-Use Radio Service)** — a license-free VHF radio band with 5 channels, a middle ground between FRS and business radios. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Mutual aid** — neighbors voluntarily helping each other based on need, without payment or hierarchy, distinct from charity (which flows one direction) or a business (which is transactional). *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

## N

**N95 / P100 respirator** — a NIOSH-certified mask that filters at least 95% (N95) or 99.97% (P100) of airborne particles when it seals tightly to the face, unlike a loose surgical or cloth mask. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Neighborhood watch** — an informal or organized group of neighbors who agree to watch out for each other's homes and streets and share information. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Net (radio)** — a scheduled, organized on-air radio session (e.g., a SKYWARN or ARES net) with a net control station coordinating traffic. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Neurotoxic venom** — venom that affects the nervous system and can impair breathing or muscle control (coral snakes and some spider venoms). *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**NOAA Weather Radio (NWR)** — a nationwide network of stations broadcasting continuous weather information and All Hazards alerts on 7 dedicated VHF frequencies, unrelated to AM/FM broadcast radio. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Nuc ("nucleus colony")** — a small, already-established mini-colony of bees, usually 4–5 frames with a laying queen, brood, and food, sold as a fast, low-risk way to start a hive. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

## O

**Offset (repeater)** — the fixed frequency gap between a radio repeater's input and output. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Open-pollinated (OP) seed** — a plant variety that, if isolated from other varieties, grows true from its own saved seed, unlike hybrids labeled "F1." *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**OPSEC (operational security)** — being careful about who knows what you have, where, and what your routines are, so that information can't be used against you or your group. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md), [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Orienting a map** — rotating a map so its "north" lines up with actual north, so map features point the same way as the real terrain. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution)** — a precise mix of water, salt, and sugar that replaces fluid and electrolytes lost to vomiting or diarrhea faster and more effectively than water alone. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md), [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**OTC** — over-the-counter medication, meaning no prescription is needed. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Out-of-area contact** — a relative or friend outside your region that separated family members all call or text to report status, since long-distance connections often work when local ones are jammed. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md), [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md), [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Outer layer / shell** — the outermost clothing layer, whose job is to block wind and shed rain or snow while ideally letting some sweat vapor escape. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

## P

**Pace count** — the number of steps (usually counted every time the same foot touches the ground) it takes to walk 100 meters; used to estimate distance traveled. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Package (bees)** — 2–3 lbs of loose worker bees plus a caged queen, with no comb or brood, shipped or sold as a way to start a hive from scratch. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Panic** — an acute stress reaction that has taken over behavior, causing actions that increase danger (running blindly, refusing help, hyperventilating to the point of fainting). *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Pasteurization** — heating milk to a specific temperature for a specific time to kill disease-causing bacteria without fully cooking it; not sterilization, so pasteurized milk still needs refrigeration. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Pathogen** — a germ that causes disease. *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md).

**PEP (post-exposure prophylaxis)** — a course of medical treatment given after a possible rabies exposure, before symptoms appear, that prevents the disease from ever developing. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Permethrin** — an insecticide made to be applied to clothing and gear (not skin) that kills and repels ticks and mosquitoes and stays effective through several washes. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Personal effects** — a deceased person's belongings found with them (ID, jewelry, clothing, phone), which should be kept with a record of the person, not scattered or discarded. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Photo of the day** — a quick phone photo of each child, taken the morning of any event with crowds, showing their exact clothing, for fast, accurate descriptions if they go missing. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

**Pit viper** — the group of venomous snakes with a heat-sensing pit between the eye and nostril, a triangular head, and elliptical pupils: copperheads, cottonmouths, and rattlesnakes. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Placard (hazmat)** — the diamond-shaped hazard label required on trucks, rail cars, and storage tanks carrying regulated hazardous materials, with a UN number identifying the exact substance. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Placard (structural, green/yellow/red tag)** — a sign a trained inspector posts on a structure after a disaster: green means inspected with no apparent hazard, yellow means restricted use, and red means unsafe to enter for any reason. An official placard, once posted, always overrides your own judgment. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Placenta (afterbirth)** — the organ that fed the baby via the umbilical cord, delivered after the baby, usually within 5–30 minutes. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**PLB (Personal Locator Beacon)** — a dedicated device that sends your GPS location to satellites and triggers a real search-and-rescue response, distinct from a phone. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Plumb** — perfectly vertical; a wall that is "out of plumb" is leaning. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Ply** — a single strand of spun fiber; "2-ply yarn" is two spun strands twisted together for strength. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**PM2.5** — fine particulate matter, 2.5 microns or smaller, the main harmful component of smoke; small enough to reach deep into the lungs, and not stopped by ordinary cloth masks. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Poisonous (vs. venomous)** — harmful if eaten or touched (some plants and toads), as opposed to venomous, which means injecting a toxin through a bite or sting. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Positive identification** — confirming a plant's identity using several independent features, not just one, ideally cross-checked against a picture guide, until there is no reasonable doubt. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Postpartum hemorrhage** — heavy bleeding after birth, the leading cause of maternal death worldwide and the top danger sign to watch for. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Potable water** — water that is safe to drink; never let it mix with greywater or blackwater. *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md), [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md), [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**PPE (personal protective equipment)** — gloves, masks/respirators, goggles, and protective clothing that reduce, but never eliminate, exposure to a hazard. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md), [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md), [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Price gouging** — charging an unfair, exploitative price for a necessity during a declared emergency; most U.S. states have statutes against this once a disaster is officially declared. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Priming a wick** — pre-soaking a raw cotton candle wick in melted wax before use, so it lights easily and burns evenly. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Priority persons** — household members whose needs must be met in full before anyone else's, even under rationing: infants, pregnant or nursing people, young children, the elderly, and the sick or injured. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Proof of loss** — a formal, signed statement of what you lost and its value, which an insurer may require to pay a claim. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md), [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Psychological First Aid (PFA)** — a structured, non-clinical way of supporting someone in acute distress: ensure safety, offer calm presence, listen without pressuring, and connect them to practical help. Not therapy or "processing the trauma." *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Pullet / cockerel** — a young female / young male chicken before full maturity. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

## Q

**Queen excluder** — a metal or plastic grid with holes sized to let worker bees pass but block the larger queen, used to keep her out of honey supers. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

## R

**Racking** — a building twisted out of its square shape, often by wind or seismic force, showing up as doors and windows that no longer close and diagonal cracks. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Radiation (heat transfer)** — heat lost as infrared energy to the cold night sky, or gained from direct sun. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Radiation (ionizing/nuclear)** — energy given off by unstable, radioactive atoms; invisible and undetectable without equipment or an official warning. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Radiator** — the vehicle part that cools hot engine coolant using outside air, with a pressurized cap on top or on a nearby overflow tank. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Rally point** — an agreed meeting place: a "near" rally point is just outside the home, a "far" rally point is outside the neighborhood, and an "out-of-town" point is a relative or friend in a different city. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md), [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md), [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Range (fuel)** — the distance a vehicle can travel on its remaining fuel, which shrinks in stop-and-go traffic, cold weather, and with a heavily loaded vehicle. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md), [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Ration / rationing** — deliberately limiting how much of something is used per person per day, usually because supply is limited and the event's end date is unknown. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Ration floor** — the minimum safe amount below which health, especially in children, the sick, and the elderly, is put at serious risk; going below it is an emergency, not a rationing strategy. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Raw milk** — milk that has not been heat-treated to kill pathogens, which may carry bacteria such as *Salmonella*, *E. coli*, or *Listeria*. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Rawhide** — animal hide that has been fleshed, dehaired, and dried under tension but not chemically tanned; stiff and durable, useful for laces, sheaths, and drum heads. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**RDA (Recommended Dietary Allowance)** — the average daily amount of a nutrient that meets the needs of nearly all healthy people, used here only as a rough planning reference. *See:* [Long-Duration Nutrition](topics/long-duration-nutrition/long-duration-nutrition.md).

**Recovery position** — lying an unconscious but breathing person on their side so the airway stays open and fluids or vomit drain out instead of being inhaled. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Re-entry** — returning to a home area after an evacuation order is lifted. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Regulator (gas)** — the device on a propane tank or gas line that controls gas pressure; damage from a knocked-over tank or storm debris can cause a leak. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**Relative value** — what something is worth right now, to two specific people, based on need and scarcity, not its normal retail price. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Remains / the deceased** — the body of a person who has died. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Rendering** — melting raw animal fat slowly and straining out the solid bits, leaving clean, storable fat (tallow or lard) for candles, soap, or cooking. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md), [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Rennet** — an enzyme, from an animal's stomach or a vegetable/microbial source, that clots milk protein into a firm curd without needing acid or long fermentation; used for most hard cheeses. *See:* [Dairy Processing](topics/dairy-processing/dairy-processing.md).

**Repeater** — a fixed radio station, usually on a tower or hilltop, that receives on one frequency and instantly retransmits on another, greatly extending range for handheld radios. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Resource pooling** — combining tools, fuel, food, or skills from multiple households for shared use, tracked so it can be returned or repaid fairly. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Resupply trigger** — a days-remaining number, decided in advance, at which you commit to restocking rather than waiting until a resource is nearly gone. *See:* [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**Retained-heat cooking (hay-box)** — bringing food to a boil, then finishing the cooking in a heavily insulated container using only the heat already in the pot, with no added fuel. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Reunification** — the formal process, used by schools, daycares, and shelters, of matching children back up with an authorized adult, usually requiring ID and a pickup list. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

**Reverse-wrap** — the core technique for twisting plant fibers into strong two-ply cordage. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Reye's syndrome** — a rare but severe illness linked to giving aspirin (or willow bark, which is chemically similar) to children or teens recovering from a viral illness. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Right side / wrong side** — the "right side" of fabric is the face meant to show; the "wrong side" is the back; seams are normally sewn right-sides-together, then turned right-side-out. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Robbing (bees)** — bees from other colonies, or wasps, invading a weak hive to steal its honey, which can wipe out the victim colony; more common in late-summer dearth. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Rotation** — periodically using and replacing stored items before they expire, so a stockpile is always close to fresh. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Roving / batt** — carded (combed straight) raw fiber, ready to spin or felt, before it's twisted into yarn. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Rule of Three (distress signal)** — the internationally recognized convention that three of anything (three fires, three whistle blasts, three shots, three flashes) signals "I need help," unlike one or two of the same thing, which reads as normal background activity. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Rule of threes (survival planning)** — a survival memory aid and planning horizon: roughly 3 minutes without air, 3 hours without shelter in harsh conditions, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food — plan your next 3 hours, then 3 days, then 3 weeks. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Rushlight** — a strip of dried plant pith dipped in fat and burned instead of a candle; the low-tech ancestor of the candle. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

## S

**Safe room / hardened room** — a single, pre-designated interior room or closet, reinforced and stocked, so a household can lock down, communicate, and wait out a threat or for help to arrive. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Safety sweep** — a first, cautious walk-through, often from outside first, to spot hazards before anyone re-enters a damaged building or starts cleanup. *See:* [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**SAME (Specific Area Message Encoding)** — the digital code NOAA Weather Radios use to alert only for a specific county or area instead of an entire region. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Sagging / bulging** — a ceiling or roof deck curving downward or ballooning, usually from water weight or broken framing; a strong sign of imminent collapse. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**SAP value (saponification value)** — how much lye a specific fat or oil needs to fully turn into soap; every fat has a different value, which is why you can't just guess quantities. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Saponification** — the chemical reaction where fat, lye, and water turn into soap and glycerin; not reversible by rinsing, it takes the right ratio and time. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Saponins** — natural soap-like compounds in certain plants (soapwort, yucca) that make suds in water without any lye or saponification at all. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Scale (forge)** — flaky black oxide that forms on hot steel; brush it off rather than hammering it in. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Scarcity** — how hard something is to get; scarcity rises fast when supply chains break, even for cheap everyday items. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Seam allowance** — the extra fabric width left between a seam line and the cut edge, so the seam doesn't pull apart. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Section road** — a farm-to-market or county road following the old mile-square survey grid; in flat country, these roads (not terrain) are the main navigation reference. *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Selvage** — the finished, non-fraying factory edge along the length of woven fabric. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Separation plan** — a pre-agreed set of actions each family member takes automatically if the family gets split up, so nobody has to invent a plan while scared. *See:* [Children & Family Operations](topics/children-family-operations/children-family-operations.md).

**Settlement (foundation)** — normal, slow sinking or shifting of a foundation over years as soil compresses; old, stable, hairline cracks are usually settlement, not disaster damage. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Shelf-stable food** — food that keeps safely at room temperature without refrigeration: canned, dried, freeze-dried, vacuum-sealed, or properly preserved. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Shelter-in-place** — staying at your current location because it is safer than moving; a chemical/smoke version means sealing a room to keep outdoor contamination or smoke out, rather than just staying put generally. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md), [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md), [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Shivering** — involuntary muscle contraction that generates heat; a good sign early on (the body is still fighting cold), but its disappearance in a cold person is a bad sign (the body is giving up). *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Shock (medical)** — a life-threatening drop in blood flow to organs, from major bleeding, severe dehydration, infection, or severe allergic reaction; not the same as an electric shock or emotional shock. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Shock chlorination** — a one-time heavy dose of chlorine used to disinfect an entire well and plumbing system after contamination. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Short circuit** — when electricity finds an unintended shortcut, such as through frayed wire touching frayed wire or through water, causing a sudden surge of current — the most common cause of tripped breakers and electrical fires. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**Shoulder (road)** — the paved or graded strip beside traffic lanes where you can safely pull off. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Signal fire** — a fire built and maintained specifically to produce visible smoke by day or flame by night as a distress signal, not for warmth or cooking. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Simplex** — direct radio-to-radio communication on the same frequency with no repeater in between; shorter range, but it doesn't depend on any infrastructure. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Situation assessment** — a fast, structured look at what's actually happening before you act, so you respond to reality instead of assumption or fear. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Situation briefing** — a short, repeated check-in during a prolonged event where the family reviews status, threats, and the plan for the next period. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Situational awareness** — actively noticing your surroundings, the people in them, and anything unusual, instead of moving through the world on autopilot. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Smoke inhalation** — lung and airway damage and poisoning from breathing smoke, heat, and combustion gases, especially carbon monoxide and, from burning plastics or foam, hydrogen cyanide. *See:* [Fire, Smoke & Air Emergencies](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md).

**Snare** — a loop of cordage or wire that tightens around an animal's neck or body as it moves through, usually triggered by the animal's own momentum. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Solder / braze / weld** — three joining strengths from weakest to strongest: solder melts only a low-temperature filler, braze melts a hotter brass/bronze filler onto un-melted base metal, and weld actually fuses the base metals together. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**SOS** — the standard distress call, in Morse code: three short, three long, three short; also used as a ground symbol. *See:* [Signaling for Rescue](topics/signaling-rescue/signaling-rescue.md).

**Span of control** — the number of people one coordinator can reasonably direct at once; CERT's rule of thumb is about 5 people per leader, with an acceptable range of 2–7. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Splint** — a rigid support used to hold a broken or badly injured limb still. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md), [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

**Standing end / standing part** — the long, static length of a rope not being manipulated while tying a knot. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**STOP (lost protocol)** — Stop, Think, Observe, Plan: the first mental drill the moment you suspect you're lost. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Stock pond / tank** — a small, man-made pond dug on rural or ranch land to water cattle, extremely common on rural parcels; locally also called a "tank." *See:* [Regional (North Texas)](topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md).

**Structural damage** — damage to the parts of a building that hold it up (foundation, walls, roof framing), as opposed to cosmetic damage (drywall, paint, flooring). *See:* [Recovery & Rebuilding](topics/recovery-rebuilding/recovery-rebuilding.md).

**Structural drying** — the deliberate use of airflow, dehumidification, and sometimes heat to remove moisture from a building's materials, not just visible puddles, to prevent mold and rot. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Structural element** — a part of a building that holds it up: foundation, load-bearing walls, framing, and the roof deck. Damage here is dangerous; damage to non-structural things like drywall or gutters is usually just repair work. *See:* [Home Structural Assessment](topics/home-structural-assessment/home-structural-assessment.md).

**Stropping** — polishing and aligning a sharpened edge on leather or cloth to remove the burr and maximize sharpness. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Suicidal ideation** — thoughts of ending one's own life, ranging from passive ("I wish I weren't here") to active with a specific plan and means; the latter is always an emergency. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Sump pump** — a pump installed in a pit in the lowest part of a basement or crawlspace that automatically ejects collected water, keeping the space dry. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Supercell** — a thunderstorm with a persistent rotating updraft (mesocyclone), the storm type responsible for most strong or violent tornadoes and large hail. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Superfat** — intentionally using slightly less lye than a fat's SAP value calls for, leaving a little unreacted fat in a finished soap bar as a safety margin against a lye-heavy bar; beginner recipes should use 5–8% superfat. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Surge / starting watts vs. running watts** — motors (fridges, well pumps, power tools) draw a big surge, 2–5 times normal, for about one second when starting, then settle to a lower running wattage; undersized generators fail at the surge moment. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Survival mindset** — the deliberate practice of staying calm, task-focused, and hopeful under extreme stress; a trainable skill, not an innate trait. *See:* [Psychological First Aid](topics/psychological-first-aid/psychological-first-aid.md).

**Suspicious or unexplained death** — any death that isn't a clearly expected, natural passing under a doctor's ongoing care; these always require police or medical-examiner involvement and should never be handled alone. *See:* [Death & Bereavement](topics/death-remains-bereavement/death-remains-bereavement.md).

**Swap / exchange event** — an organized gathering where several households bring items to trade, rather than trading one-on-one. *See:* [Bartering & Trade](topics/bartering-trade-exchange/bartering-trade-exchange.md).

**Swarm** — roughly half a bee colony, with the old queen, leaving to found a new nest elsewhere — the colony's natural way of reproducing. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Systemic infection / sepsis** — an infection that has spread beyond its original site into the bloodstream or whole body; life-threatening and needs emergency care. *See:* [Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md).

## T

**Tallow** — rendered fat from cattle or sheep, hard and long-lasting; used as a traditional candle, lamp, and soap fuel. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md), [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Tannins** — bitter, astringent compounds in acorns, oak bark, and other plants; not usually deadly in small amounts but cause stomach upset, and removed by leaching (soaking or boiling in changed water). *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Target hardening vs. target denial** — hardening makes entry harder; denial, via OPSEC, avoids looking worth targeting at all. Denial is cheaper and usually more effective. *See:* [Security & Defense](topics/security-defense/security-defense.md).

**Temper** — a gentler reheat after hardening steel that trades a little hardness for toughness; skipping it leaves tools dangerously brittle. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Tiered readiness** — building supplies in stages (72-hour, then 2-week, then 30-day) so you always have a complete, usable level of coverage rather than a half-finished pile. *See:* [Supplies & Inventory Rotation](topics/supplies-inventory-rotation/supplies-inventory-rotation.md).

**Tinder** — the finest, most flammable fire-starting material, which should catch a small spark or ember and burst into flame within seconds (dry grass, dead pine needles, birch bark). *See:* [Fire](topics/fire/fire.md).

**Tippy-tap** — a simple, hands-free handwashing device (a hanging water container tipped by a foot pedal or string) that uses very little water and avoids contaminating the water supply with dirty hands. *See:* [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**Title (vehicle)** — the legal document of ownership for a vehicle, or informally, other property. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Toxin** — a poisonous substance produced by a plant, mushroom, or insect; some act within minutes, while delayed toxins cause damage before symptoms appear, making them the most dangerous kind. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Tourniquet** — a wide strap tightened above a wound to physically stop all blood flow to a limb when direct pressure has failed to stop life-threatening bleeding. *See:* [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md).

**Trace (soap)** — the point during soap-making when the batter has thickened enough that a drizzle from a spoon leaves a visible trail on the surface for a second before sinking back in; this is when you pour it into the mold. *See:* [Soap & Hygiene Making](topics/soap-and-hygiene-making/soap-and-hygiene-making.md).

**Traction** — the grip between a tire and the surface beneath it, lost in mud, sand, ice, and snow. *See:* [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Transfer switch** — a device installed by a licensed electrician that lets a generator power selected house circuits while mechanically guaranteeing the house is disconnected from the utility grid, making backfeed impossible. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md), [Structural & Utility Emergencies](topics/structural-utility-emergencies/structural-utility-emergencies.md).

**Transplant** — a young seedling moved from a pot or tray into its growing bed. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Trench foot (immersion foot)** — tissue damage from feet being wet and cold, not necessarily freezing, for hours to days. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Triage** — sorting problems, or people, by urgency so the most life-threatening ones are addressed first; borrowed from medicine and applied here to decisions generally. *See:* [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

**Trichinosis** — a parasitic roundworm disease from undercooked pork or wild hog (and some other meat) that can be serious or fatal; killed by thorough cooking. *See:* [Hunting, Fishing & Trapping](topics/food-hunting-fishing-trapping/food-hunting-fishing-trapping.md).

**Trip (breaker)** — when a breaker automatically shuts off because it detected too much current or a short circuit, protecting the wiring from overheating. *See:* [Electrical, Gas & Utility Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md).

**True north** — the direction to the geographic North Pole, where lines of longitude meet; maps are drawn to true north. *See:* [Navigation](topics/navigation/navigation.md).

**Tunneling (candle)** — when a candle burns a narrow hole straight down the middle and leaves solid wax untouched on the sides, wasting most of the candle, usually because the wick is too small for the candle's diameter. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Turbidity** — cloudiness in water from suspended particles; cloudy water needs to be cleared before disinfecting, or germs can "hide" inside the particles. *See:* [Water](topics/water/water.md).

**Turn Around, Don't Drown** — the National Weather Service's flash-flood safety slogan: never drive or walk into water of unknown depth or any moving water, ever. *See:* [Disaster-Specific Guides](topics/disaster-specific/disaster-specific.md), [Transportation & Vehicle Emergencies](topics/transportation-vehicle-emergencies/transportation-vehicle-emergencies.md).

**Tuyere** — the pipe or opening that feeds air into a forge fire. *See:* [Metalwork & Toolmaking](topics/metalwork-and-toolmaking/metalwork-and-toolmaking.md).

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)** — a login that requires a password plus a second proof, such as a code texted to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

## U

**Umbel** — an umbrella-shaped flower cluster where many small flower stalks radiate from one point; the carrot family, identified partly by this shape, includes both wild carrot and the two deadliest plants in North America. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

**Universal Edibility Test (UET)** — a long, staged process for testing an unknown plant part when no positive identification is possible and starvation is a real risk; slow and imperfect, and never to be used on mushrooms. *See:* [Foraging & Wild Edibles](topics/food-foraging-wild-edibles/food-foraging-wild-edibles.md).

## V

**Vapor barrier liner (VBL)** — a waterproof layer, such as a plastic bag, worn between two sock or glove layers in cold conditions to stop sweat from soaking the insulation, at the cost of a damp layer against the skin. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Vapor density** — whether a gas is heavier or lighter than air; heavier gases (like chlorine) sink and pool in low spots, while lighter gases rise and disperse faster. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Varroa mite** (*Varroa destructor*) — a parasitic mite that feeds on bees and spreads viruses; the leading cause of colony death worldwide. *See:* [Beekeeping](topics/beekeeping/beekeeping.md).

**Vasodilation / vasoconstriction** — blood vessels near the skin widening to dump heat when you're hot, and narrowing to conserve heat when you're cold. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Vector** — an animal or insect that carries and transmits disease between hosts, such as ticks, mosquitoes, rodents, and flies. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md), [Sanitation & Hygiene](topics/sanitation-hygiene/sanitation-hygiene.md).

**Vehicle emergency kit** — supplies that live in a vehicle permanently, separate from a go-bag, for roadside problems and being stranded. *See:* [Evacuation & Bug-Out](topics/evacuation-bugout-vehicle/evacuation-bugout-vehicle.md).

**Veering wind** — wind shifting clockwise, usually signaling a front has passed and weather is improving. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Venomous** — an animal that injects a toxin through a bite or sting, such as snakes, spiders, and some insects. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).

**Vernix** — the white, waxy coating on a newborn's skin; it protects the skin and should not be scrubbed off. *See:* [Childbirth & Vulnerable Care](topics/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care/childbirth-and-vulnerable-care.md).

**Vital records** — birth, marriage, and death certificates, Social Security cards, passports, and similar government-issued core documents. *See:* [Documents & Money](topics/documents-money-admin/documents-money-admin.md).

**Volatile / off-gassing** — a chemical that evaporates into breathable fumes at room temperature, such as many solvents, fuels, and ammonia. *See:* [Hazmat & Contamination](topics/hazmat-contamination/hazmat-contamination.md).

**Volt (V)** — electrical "pressure": USB devices use 5V, most portable and car batteries use 12V, and US house outlets use 120V AC. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

## W

**Wall cloud** — a lowered, rotating cloud base beneath a thunderstorm, often the visual sign of a developing tornado. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Warm front** — warm air overriding cold air and sliding up gradually, producing a slow, layered cloud sequence over 12–36 hours ending in steady rain. *See:* [Weather Prediction](topics/weather-prediction/weather-prediction.md).

**Warm-season / heat-tolerant crop** — a crop needing warm soil and killed by frost, such as tomato, pepper, squash, or okra. *See:* [Food Growing](topics/food-growing/food-growing.md).

**Warp / weft** — in woven fabric, warp threads run the length (usually stronger, less stretchy) while weft threads run across, woven over-and-under the warp. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

**Water activity (Aw)** — how much "free" water is in food for bacteria to use; drying, salting, and sugaring all work by lowering water activity below the point where most bacteria can grow. *See:* [Food Preservation & Cooking](topics/food-preservation-cooking/food-preservation-cooking.md).

**Water hammer** — the banging noise or pressure spike when a valve closes suddenly, which can stress old pipes. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Water table** — the underground level below which soil is saturated; a high water table pushes groundwater into basements and crawlspaces. *See:* [Water, Flooding & Plumbing](topics/water-flooding-plumbing/water-flooding-plumbing.md).

**Watt (W)** — a unit of power: how fast energy is being used or produced right now (a phone charger might use 5–20 W, a refrigerator 100–800 W). *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md).

**Watt-hour (Wh)** — energy over time, watts multiplied by hours; the number that tells you how long something can run off a given battery. *See:* [Power & Energy](topics/power-energy/power-energy.md), [Survival Logistics & Resources](topics/survival-logistics-resources/survival-logistics-resources.md).

**WEA (Wireless Emergency Alert)** — the loud, distinct-tone alert a cell phone can receive even without a data or voice plan, as long as the tower is up and the phone is registered to a network. *See:* [Communication](topics/communication/communication.md).

**Weaning** — when young animals stop nursing and move to solid feed only. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Wellness check** — a short, respectful visit or call to confirm a person is safe, has what they need, and knows how to reach the group. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Wick** — the string or cord that draws melted wax or liquid fuel up to a flame by capillary action and controls how the fuel burns. *See:* [Lighting & Candles](topics/lighting-and-candles/lighting-and-candles.md).

**Wicking** — moving moisture (sweat) away from skin into fabric, where it can evaporate, instead of pooling on the skin. *See:* [Clothing & Footwear](topics/clothing-footwear-protection/clothing-footwear-protection.md).

**Widowmaker** — a dead branch or tree that can fall without warning; never camp or sit directly under one. *See:* [Shelter](topics/shelter/shelter.md).

**Wind chill** — how much faster exposed skin loses heat because of wind; it doesn't lower the temperature of objects, only how fast a body loses heat. *See:* [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md).

**Withdrawal period** — the time after giving an animal medication before its meat, milk, or eggs are safe to consume; critical whenever dewormers or antibiotics are used. *See:* [Livestock & Animal Husbandry](topics/livestock-animal-husbandry/livestock-animal-husbandry.md).

**Working end** — the end of a rope you are actively tying with. *See:* [Tools, Knots & Repair](topics/tools-knots-repair/tools-knots-repair.md).

**Work rotation** — a schedule that spreads necessary but tiring jobs (watch duty, hauling water, cooking, childcare) across multiple people so no one burns out. *See:* [Community & Mutual Aid](topics/community-mutual-aid/community-mutual-aid.md).

**Woven fabric** — fabric made by interlacing two sets of threads at right angles, like denim or canvas; it doesn't stretch much, and frayed edges unravel. *See:* [Textiles & Clothing Making](topics/textiles-and-clothing-making/textiles-and-clothing-making.md).

## Z

**Zoonotic disease** — a disease that spreads from animals to humans, such as rabies, tick-borne illnesses, hantavirus, and West Nile virus. *See:* [Animal, Insect & Pest Hazards](topics/animal-insect-pest-hazards/animal-insect-pest-hazards.md).
