# START HERE — Emergency Quick-Reference & Guide Router

**If this is a life-threatening emergency and phones work, call 911 first.** This page is for when help is delayed or unreachable. Read the 30 seconds below, then jump to the guide you need.

---

## ⛑️ STEP 1 — Immediate life threats (handle these first, in order)

Check the person/scene for these **now**. Each links to the exact section you need.

| If… | Do this first | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Not breathing / no pulse** | Start CPR: hard & fast center of chest, 100–120/min, 2 in deep | [First Aid → CPR](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md) |
| **Severe bleeding** (spurting/pooling) | Press hard on the wound; tourniquet high-and-tight on a limb if needed | [First Aid → Bleeding](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md) |
| **Choking** (can't breathe/speak) | Back blows + abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) | [First Aid → Choking](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md) |
| **Not alert / unresponsive but breathing** | Recovery position; keep airway open; monitor | [First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md) |
| **Smell gas / hissing** | Don't flip switches — get everyone out, then call gas utility from outside | [Electrical/Gas Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md) |
| **Fire / smoke in building** | Get out, stay out, meet at your spot; crawl low under smoke | [Fire, Smoke & Air](topics/fire-smoke-air-emergencies/fire-smoke-air-emergencies.md) |
| **Downed power line** | Stay back ≥35 ft (10 ft min); never touch it or anything it touches | [Electrical/Gas Safety](topics/electrical-gas-utility-safety/electrical-gas-utility-safety.md) |
| **Signs of heat stroke** (hot, confused, collapse) | Cool aggressively — shade, water, ice on neck/armpits/groin | [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md) |
| **Signs of hypothermia** (shivering→confusion) | Get dry, insulate, warm gently, handle gently | [Extreme Heat & Cold](topics/extreme-heat-cold/extreme-heat-cold.md) |

More: shock, burns, fractures, allergic reaction/anaphylaxis, poisoning → **[First Aid](topics/first-aid/first-aid.md)** and **[Emergency Medicine](topics/emergency-medicine-herbal/emergency-medicine-herbal.md)**. Poison Control (US): **1-800-222-1222**. Mental-health crisis: **988**.

---

## 🧭 STEP 2 — Remember the survival priorities (the Rule of 3s)

You can survive roughly: **3 minutes** without air/severe bleeding controlled · **3 hours** without shelter in harsh cold/heat · **3 days** without water · **3 weeks** without food. **Work the list in that order** — don't chase food while you're freezing or dehydrated.

Then make the **two universal decisions**: **(1) shelter in place or evacuate?** and **(2) stay put or move to get found?** → [Emergency Planning & Triage](topics/emergency-planning-triage/emergency-planning-triage.md).

## For the offline AI assistant (JARVIS)
When someone asks a survival question, use the STEP 1 table to rule out any immediate life threat first, then use STEP 3 to open the matching topic guide(s) and answer from their step-by-step sections. Prefer the specific guide over general advice, give more than one method when the guide offers them, state safety warnings, and tell the person to get professional help the moment it's reachable. For location-specific answers in TX/OK/AR, also pull `reference/local/<state>.md`.

> ⚠️ This is educational reference for when professional help isn't available. It is not a substitute for 911, a doctor, or trained responders. When you can reach them, do.
