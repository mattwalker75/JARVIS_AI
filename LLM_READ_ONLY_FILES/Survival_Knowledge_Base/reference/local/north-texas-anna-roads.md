# Anna / Collin & Grayson County — Roads, Landmarks & Egress Sheet

This sheet exists so you can answer **"where am I and how do I get out"** from memory or a single page —
**without** having to open and read a 30–60 MB topographic PDF first. For the actual maps, see
[North Texas Maps — Coverage Index](../maps/north-texas/COVERAGE.md). Orient yourself first using the
[Navigation Without GPS](../../topics/navigation/navigation.md) guide (this is flat country — you
navigate by the **road grid**, not by hills).

## Where Anna sits
**Anna, TX (75409)** is in **north-central Collin County**, about **11 miles northeast of McKinney**,
strung along **US-75**. Three roads define the town:
- **US-75 (Central Expressway)** — the main **north–south** freeway, runs right through Anna. **South = toward McKinney, Plano, Dallas (DFW). North = toward Van Alstyne, Sherman, Denison, and the Oklahoma border.**
- **SH 5** — the older highway that **parallels US-75** just to the east through downtown; the local
  street-level route between McKinney, Melissa, Anna, and Van Alstyne when the freeway is blocked.
- **FM 455** — the main **east–west** road through Anna (it's the town's main cross street, running from
  **SH 5 west across US-75** toward Celina/Pilot Point and **east** toward Blue Ridge/Westminster).

Downtown Anna sits around the **SH 5 / FM 455** intersection, just east of the US-75 freeway, with the
**BNSF railroad** running alongside SH 5/US-75. The **Collin County Outer Loop** (a partial beltway) is
building across the southern part of the county.

## The four ways out (cardinal egress routes)
Think of getting out as one of four directions on US-75 or FM 455:

- **SOUTH → DFW metro (US-75 south).** Anna → Melissa (~5 mi) → **McKinney (~11 mi)** → Allen → Plano →
  **Dallas (~45 mi)**. This is the fastest route to major hospitals, supplies, and the airport, but the
  **most likely to be congested** in a mass evacuation. Parallel backups: **SH 5** and **SH 121 (Sam
  Rayburn Tollway)** to the south. Map: `txdot_dallas_district.pdf`, `txdot_collin_county.pdf`.
- **NORTH → Oklahoma (US-75 north).** Anna → **Van Alstyne (~7 mi)** → Howe → **Sherman (~22 mi)** →
  **Denison (~30 mi)** → **cross the Red River into Oklahoma (Colbert → Durant)**. This is the route
  **away from the DFW population** and toward the Red River / Lake Texoma. Maps: the `ustopo_*` corridor
  quads (Dorchester, Gunter, Collinsville, Sherman, Denison Dam, Gordonville) and `txdot_paris_district.pdf`.
- **EAST → rural Collin/Fannin (FM 455 east / SH 121 northeast).** Toward Blue Ridge, Westminster, and
  **Bonham (Fannin County)**. Thinly populated farm country — good for avoiding crowds, fewer services.
- **WEST → US-377 / Celina / Pilot Point (FM 455 west).** Toward Celina, Pilot Point, **Denton**, and
  the west side of the metro; connects to **US-377** north to **Lake Texoma's west end** and **I-35**.

## Hospitals & emergency rooms (verified)
- **In Anna:** **ER at Anna** (a freestanding ER, service of Texoma Medical Center), **2710 Hackberry
  Dr, Anna 75409** — 24-hour emergency care right in town. Good for stabilization; not a full hospital.
- **South (nearest full hospitals, ~11–15 mi via US-75):** **Medical City McKinney** and **Baylor Scott
  & White Medical Center – McKinney** (McKinney) — full-service hospitals with trauma capability.
- **North (~22–30 mi via US-75):** **ER at Sherman** and **Texoma Medical Center (Denison)** — the main
  hospital for the Sherman/Denison/Red River area.
> For a true life-threat, call **911**; if phones are down, the fastest full hospital is **south on
> US-75 to McKinney.** See [First Aid](../../topics/first-aid/first-aid.md) and the
> [Texas reference sheet](texas.md) for trauma-center and contact details.

## Water bodies (see the [Regional North Texas guide](../../topics/regional-north-texas/regional-north-texas.md) for treating any of it)
- **East Fork of the Trinity River** — drains most of Collin County, flowing generally **south-southeast**
  (a reliable way to know which way is "downhill/downstream" in flat country). Feeds Lavon Lake.
- **Lavon Lake** — large reservoir **~15 mi south** (via SH 78 / FM 6 area); the region's big surface-water
  source to the south.
- **Lake Texoma** — very large reservoir on the **Red River ~30 mi north** (US-75 to Denison, then west);
  marks the Oklahoma border. Quads: `ustopo_pottsboro_tx.pdf`, `ustopo_denison_dam_tx.pdf`, `ustopo_gordonville_tx.pdf`.
- **Stock ponds** — nearly every rural property near Anna has one; **treat all as contaminated** (cattle
  runoff, algae) until cleared and disinfected.

## Red River crossings into Oklahoma (nearest first)
- **US-75 at Denison** — the **primary, closest** crossing; over the Red River near **Denison Dam / Lake
  Texoma** into **Colbert, OK**, continuing to **Durant, OK**. (~33 mi north of Anna.)
- **US-377** — west end of Lake Texoma, crosses toward **Kingston/Madill, OK** (a westerly alternate).
- **US-82** — far **east**, from the **Paris, TX** area into **Hugo, OK** (a fallback if the US-75 bridge
  is blocked). Covered by `txdot_paris_district.pdf`.

## Landmarks you navigate by (flat-country cues)
- **US-75** and the **BNSF railroad** run **north–south** together — find either and you know your N–S line.
- **Water towers** in each town (Anna, Melissa, Van Alstyne) are visible for miles and mark town centers.
- **Highway overpasses** are the **highest ground** for miles — useful lookouts and flood refuges when no
  natural high ground exists.
- **Section-line farm roads** run on a **north–south / east–west grid** (½- and 1-mile spacing) — a moving
  count of them estimates distance traveled.
- **FM 455** is your main **east–west** reference through Anna; **US-75/SH 5** your main **north–south** one.
